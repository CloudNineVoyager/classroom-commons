# 为全球乡村教师而做 / For rural teachers worldwide

课堂共作想做一件具体的事：让全球乡村教师用已有的时间和材料，更容易备好一节课。不是只有中国的乡村教师才需要它，也不假设各地的困难完全相同。

Classroom Commons helps rural teachers worldwide plan a lesson with the time and materials they actually have. It is not limited to one country, and rural classrooms are not all alike.

## 困境与我们能帮的部分 / Problems and practical support

师资与支持不足、复式班和材料有限，是国际研究记录的一部分困难；不同国家、地区和学校的程度与组合各不相同。OECD讨论了农村小校的复式教学、教学材料和教师同伴支持，UNESCO报告讨论了全球教师短缺。[OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/responsive-school-systems_9789264306707-en/full-report/component-8.html) · [UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/global-report-teachers-addressing-teacher-shortages-and-transforming-profession)

International evidence describes staffing constraints, multigrade teaching, limited materials and professional isolation, with substantial variation between places. See the OECD and UNESCO sources above. This software addresses a small part of the planning burden, not the underlying staffing or funding gap.

## 永久免费承诺 / Free, now and in future updates

项目和官方更新坚持永久免费、MIT开源，不设付费版，不以账号、订阅或API解锁基础功能。老师可保留和分享离线文件。自愿使用的第三方AI或API可能收费，这不是本项目收费，也不会成为继续编辑和导出的条件。MIT原有授权不因未来维护变化而撤回。

The project and official updates will remain free and MIT licensed, without a paid tier or account/API gate. Teachers can keep and share offline copies. Optional outside AI/API services may charge; they are not required for local editing or export. Existing MIT rights remain available if maintenance changes.

## 下一步 / Next

优先做教师真实反馈、更多少材料案例、无障碍与低配设备检查、社区校对的翻译；再考虑经教师审阅的本地活动模板。它们是路线图，不是已经实现的功能。真实乡村教师课堂试用尚未完成，程序检查通过不等于教学效果获证。[维护与更新约定](MAINTENANCE.md)

Next priorities are teacher feedback, material-light examples, accessibility and lower-end device checks, community-reviewed translations, then locally reviewed activity templates. These are plans, not shipped features. Rural classroom trials remain pending; passing software checks is not proof of learning outcomes. [Maintenance commitments](MAINTENANCE.md)
